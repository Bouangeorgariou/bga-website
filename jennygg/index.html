Why inclusive matters to me 🌈

Everyone deserves to feel seen on their day.

I came out and came of age in the '80s, when it was hard to see myself represented at a wedding. That stayed with me.

So I photograph for every couple who's ever felt left out — gay, LGBTQ+, and anyone who just wants to be exactly themselves. On your day, that's what you'll be: yourselves, celebrated, and completely seen.
