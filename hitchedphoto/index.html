Worth knowing

You'll spend more of the day with us than with each other.

That's not a joke, it's just how a wedding day works. Which means the single most useful thing you can do when you're choosing a photographer is decide whether you'd actually enjoy having them around for twelve hours.

We're light hearted, we're fans of run-on sentences and mildly inappropriate humour, and we bring a lot of positive energy. We also put a capital P on professionalism: prompt replies from the first inquiry to well past the day you say I do.

Aside from everything we have to tell you about ourselves, the most important thing here is you.

Why it's all one brand

Three cameras in the room.
No crossed wires.

Most couples hire a photographer, then a separate video team, then a photo booth company, and hope on the day that all three get along. We do all three ourselves, which means one workflow, one timeline, and nobody stepping into anybody else's shot.
