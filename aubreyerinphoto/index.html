What I believe

The best moments don't happen on command.

They happen in the in-between. When you're laughing without realising it. When your kid reaches for your hand. When your partner squeezes your fingers during the ceremony. Those are the ones that deserve to be remembered, exactly as they were.

So I'll ask a lot of questions to get to know you, then give you room to actually be with each other. Gentle direction, and then I get out of the way.

You don't need to perform. You don't need to be "photogenic." You just need to show up as you are.

About me

Hi, I'm Aubrey.

I'm a documentary wedding, family and portrait photographer in Kansas City, serving couples and families across the greater metro and well beyond it. I've been doing this for over fifteen years, and I still love it because every session, couple, family and person I meet is completely different.

I thrive on discovering beauty in every person and relationship I get to photograph. I'll have lots of questions to get to know you, and I'll also give you room to just connect with each other. If you're the sort of person who wants to be micromanaged, I'm probably not your photographer. If you want your life remembered the way it actually felt, I am.
