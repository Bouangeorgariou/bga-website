Who this is for

The couples I photograph never got too serious.

At their wedding, they are fully alive and happy to be there. Their joy is contagious, and before long everyone around them feels it too. They want a wedding their way, the fun way, the way that feels like them. If a tradition doesn't fit, it's out, or modified until it does.

They spent a year building a day that reflects who they are, and the last thing they want is to be pulled away from the fun for photos. They want to stay immersed in it, surrounded by the people they love.

"He blended in so naturally that people kept asking if he was a friend or relative of ours. He managed to capture genuine smiles, emotional moments, and all the little details we didn't even realize were happening." Cierra · Kansas City wedding

The experience

It starts with a rendez-vous.

Preferably in a coffee shop or a bar, because that makes it a little special. Everything after that is designed so you never have to think about photography again.

01

We meet properly

A rendez-vous where we talk about your vision, your interests, your values — and whether we click. We usually do.

02

We lock the date

Then we stay in touch through the planning, often with a couple's session along the way, and meet again before the big day.

03

The day itself

I move the plastic water bottle out of frame and otherwise leave your day alone. No staging after the fact — it is a waste of time you should be spending with your guests.

04

The album reveal

I select and edit with care, then craft an album. Four to six weeks later we meet again to reveal the photographs and fine-tune it together.
