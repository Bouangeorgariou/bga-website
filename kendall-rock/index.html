Start from scratch with me

Who are you, together?

I love working from the ground up. Why are you getting married? What actually matters emotionally on the day, if you erase the list of traditions you were told to include? Then we slot the photo logistics into that vision, and use them to make the day flow better.

I am here to help manifest your dream, not to reproduce somebody else's.

Some of my favourite things couples have done
  • Dinner before the ceremony
  • Guest participation in the vows
  • Noise makers, so people cheer during the ceremony
  • A live karaoke band
  • A polar plunge with the team to start the day
  • Potluck dinner
  • Handmade everything
Your right hand ma'am

There are only two jobs that day.

My job

Being a calming presence at your side all day while secretly keeping track of time, politely swatting away anyone sucking up your precious energy, turning treasured moments into images, creating space for stillness when I sense you need it, and capturing the things you aren't noticing.

Your job

Be present, soak it in, and enjoy all the hard work you've put into planning the dang thing.

Me

Born and bred in the Pacific Northwest.

I am Kendall. Seattle-based wedding, elopement and music tour photographer, and I am almost always on the road — Washington, Alaska, the greater PNW and considerably beyond.

I book one year in advance. It keeps my sanity and gives me a little control over my schedule; my actual nightmare is being booked to photograph a wedding on the day my best friend schedules hers. You are welcome to reach out earlier and I will pencil you in, I just can't confirm until a year out.
