The approach

You should not have to perform at your own wedding.

If the idea of being watched all day sounds exhausting, you are not alone. A lot of our couples want beautiful photos and films without feeling like they have to put on a show.

So we pay attention to what actually matters — the in-between moments, the weird jokes, the deep breaths, the happy nerves, the people you love most. We step back while things unfold, and step in with simple guidance when it helps. You will never be over-posed, rushed, or left wondering what to do.

We care less about perfection, and more about making sure your photos feel like you.

The DePanghers

We met at a networking meeting and ruined the plan.

We're Eric (he/him) and Amber (she/her). Before we met, we each ran our own established wedding photography business, with years of experience already behind us. We met at a wedding industry meeting, became friends, and — unexpectedly — fell in love about a month later. After shooting a full season separately, we merged the two businesses into Sightglass.

We're big believers in clear timelines and thoughtful time-blocking, which means we never overbook, never rush, and can give every couple the attention they deserve. We work with all kinds of couples and all kinds of weddings — big or small, formal or casual, loud or low-key. We proudly work with LGBTQ+ couples, and we're body-inclusive: everyone deserves to feel good in front of a camera.

How it works

Easy to book, easier on the day.

You don't need to have everything figured out yet. Most couples start with a date and a rough idea — we can go from there.
